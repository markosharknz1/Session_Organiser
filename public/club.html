Other computers

A second computer at the desk can check people in, and a TV or tablet can show the External Display, by opening this computer's Game Scheduler over the club's network. Everything stays in one place: this computer keeps the only copy of the club's data, and what is done on one screen shows on the others straight away.

Access PIN

Every other device has to enter this PIN once before it can open Game Scheduler; it then stays signed in. This computer is never asked. Other devices can't connect at all until a PIN is set.

Email

Connection details for sending the end-of-night summary. Staff click "Send summary" after finishing a session - nothing is ever sent automatically.

Summaries are sent with: -

You can fill in more than one provider. Only the one flagged Default is used to send.

SMTP2Go - smtp2go.com

You will need:

  • An SMTP2Go account.
  • The club's own domain name, such as sportsclub.com.
  • Access to that domain's DNS settings, to add the records SMTP2Go gives you to verify the domain as a sender.
  • An API key from the SMTP2Go account, allowed to send email.

The sender email below must be an address at the verified domain, for example sessions@sportsclub.com.

Mailgun - mailgun.com

You will need:

  • A Mailgun account.
  • The club's own domain name, such as sportsclub.com.
  • Access to that domain's DNS settings, to add the records Mailgun gives you to verify the sending domain (often a subdomain such as mg.sportsclub.com).
  • A sending API key from the Mailgun account.

The sending domain must be in Mailgun's US region - that is the one this app sends through. The sender email should be an address at that domain.

Gmail - gmail.com

You will need:

  • A Gmail (Google) account for the club. No domain name or DNS changes are needed - the simplest choice for a club without its own domain.
  • 2-Step Verification turned on for that account.
  • An App Password, created at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. This is not the account's normal password.

Summaries arrive from that Gmail address.
